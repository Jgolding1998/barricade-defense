Shape the fight

Needles beat scouts. Mortars break groups and armor. Chill towers keep runners under fire. Enemies get much tougher each wave, so towers alone will not hold.

Spend with intent

Barricades start at 9 and rise by 1 for each one standing. Selling recovers 60%. Upgrade a strong position before opening another one. A complete blockade is rejected.
